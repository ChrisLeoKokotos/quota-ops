# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in QuotaOps, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- being respectful and constructive;
- assuming good faith while still reviewing critically;
- accepting feedback professionally;
- focusing disagreements on ideas, code, evidence, and outcomes;
- respecting privacy and security boundaries;
- helping maintain a productive technical community.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discrimination;
- personal attacks or sustained hostile behavior;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberately submitting malicious code, credential-stealing behavior, hidden telemetry, or other deceptive changes;
- knowingly disrupting project infrastructure or community spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, lock, or restrict contributions or participation that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or sensitive conduct concerns should be reported privately to the maintainer using a private contact method available through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not disclose private or sensitive information in a public issue.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and to public conduct when an individual is representing QuotaOps or its community.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by common open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant.
